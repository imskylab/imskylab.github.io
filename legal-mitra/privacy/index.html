Legal Mitra — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 October 2026

Legal Mitra is an Indian legal information app for Android and iOS, created and developed by Jayvardhan Potabatti ("I", "me"). Contact: skylabs.in@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Legal Mitra has no servers and no accounts. I do not receive your data.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • What you create stays encrypted on your phone.
  • Anything that leaves your phone goes only where you choose to send it: your own cloud storage, Google Classroom or an AI provider. It goes directly from your phone to that service, under that service's own terms.

What is stored on your phone

When you create a local profile, the app stores on your device:

  • your profile and settings;
  • matters, parties, hearings, tasks, deadlines and the matter activity log;
  • notes, bookmarks, research folders, saved searches and search history (search history can be turned off and cleared);
  • documents you import or scan, the text recognised from them, and your annotations;
  • learning progress, flashcards, quiz attempts, case briefs and teaching material;
  • citizen help journeys and legal-literacy progress.

This data is kept in a database encrypted with SQLCipher, and document files are encrypted with AES-GCM. The encryption keys are held in the Android Keystore or the iOS Keychain. The app turns off Android's automatic cloud backup and device-transfer copies of its data.

Text recognition (OCR) runs on the device. Your documents are not uploaded for it.

Uninstalling the app, or using Me → Delete local profile, deletes this data and its keys from the phone.

Exporting your data

Me → Privacy → Export my data makes one file with the kinds of data you choose, encrypted with a passphrase you set (the app does not keep the passphrase). You decide where the file goes, using your phone's share sheet; the app's temporary copy is deleted afterwards. You can restore the file into Legal Mitra on any phone. Without the passphrase nobody, including me, can open it.

Google ML Kit

On-device text recognition and the Android document scanner use Google ML Kit. The documents and their text stay on your phone. Google states that ML Kit may send Google anonymous usage and performance information, and may download updated models. This is governed by Google's terms: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms

On Android, documents are chosen with the system file picker and scanned with the system scanner, so the app does not request camera or storage permissions.

Legal content

Acts, sections, judgments and other legal material are installed on the phone as signed content packages. Reading and searching them happens on the device and sends nothing anywhere.

Optional services you connect

These are off until you set them up. Each connects your phone directly to the provider; I never see the data or the credentials. The provider's own privacy policy and terms apply.

Cloud backup and sync (Google Drive, Microsoft OneDrive, Dropbox). Backups and sync data are encrypted on your phone before upload, with a recovery key that only you hold. They are stored in the app's own folder in your account. I cannot read them. Legal Mitra does not sell storage; your provider's storage terms and charges apply.

Google Classroom. If you connect it, the app asks Google only for the permissions the feature needs. It shows your classes and coursework but does not keep a copy of them. You can disconnect at any time.

AI assistance with your own key (OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic, Ollama or an OpenAI-compatible server). AI is off by default. When you use an AI feature, the text it needs (for example a section, a judgment or your question) goes directly from your phone to the provider you chose. Your own documents or matters are sent only after the app shows you exactly what will be sent and you confirm. Matter review never includes client names or contact details. Your API key is stored in the phone's secure storage. You pay the provider directly; Legal Mitra does not sell AI credits.

Sign-in to these services uses the provider's own sign-in page in your browser. Access tokens are kept in the phone's secure storage and are removed when you disconnect or delete your local profile.

Purchases

Legal Mitra Pro is bought through Google Play or the Apple App Store, which process the payment. The app learns only whether you own Pro. I do not receive your payment details.

Children

Legal Mitra is intended for users aged 18 and over.

Your choices

  • Export a copy of your data in one encrypted file (Me → Privacy → Export my data).
  • Delete everything on the phone (Me → Delete local profile), or uninstall the app.
  • Disconnect cloud storage, Google Classroom or an AI provider at any time, and remove your AI key.
  • Turn off and clear search history.

Copies you have placed in your own cloud storage or in a connected service stay there until you delete them in that service.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same address with a new effective date.
