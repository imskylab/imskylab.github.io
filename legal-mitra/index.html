What it does

For legal practitioners, law students, teachers and citizens. Each sees the tools that fit.

The law, with its history

Acts and sections with dated versions, a legal dictionary and old-to-new law mappings, each showing its official source.

Judgments and citations

Search cases, read judgments by paragraph, and see which later decisions cite, follow or overrule them.

Research

Fast search on the phone, saved searches, bookmarks, notes and a citation tracker.

Matters and reminders

Matters, hearings, tasks and deadlines with reminders, all kept on the phone.

Documents

Import or scan documents, recognise their text on the device, and annotate them.

Learning and help

Lessons, flashcards and quizzes for students, teaching tools, and plain-language guidance for citizens.

Private by design

There is no Legal Mitra server and no account.

Your work stays on your phone

Matters, notes and documents are encrypted on the device. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose an optional service.

Optional services are yours

Back up to your own Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox (encrypted before upload), connect Google Classroom, or use AI with your own provider key. Each connects your phone directly to that service.

Please note

Legal information, not legal advice

Legal Mitra explains the law and helps you organise your work. It does not replace a lawyer. For a decision about your own case, speak to an advocate or a legal services authority.

Not affiliated with any government body

Legal Mitra is an independent app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government, court or authority. It uses public material from official sources such as India Code (Legislative Department, Ministry of Law and Justice), the Supreme Court of India, eCourts Services and the National Legal Services Authority, and shows the source of every text.
