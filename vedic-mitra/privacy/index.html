Privacy Policy — Vedic Mitra

Effective date: 17 September 2026 · Applies to: the Vedic Mitra Android application

The short version

Vedic Mitra collects nothing, sends nothing to its developer, and has no servers. There is no account, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. Everything you enter — your birth details, saved locations, reminders, questionnaire answers, japa and meditation history — stays in the app's private storage on your device, and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

The app contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting library, and no network client of its own.

If you buy Vedic Mitra Pro, Google Play sells it and handles the payment. The app learns only whether Pro is owned, and remembers that on your device.

What the app stores, and where

All of it lives in the app's private on-device storage (Android DataStore). None of it is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Permissions and sensors, and exactly what each is for

  • Location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION) — optional. Used only to work out your coordinates, so sunrise, tithi and muhurta timings, and the compass's correction to true north, are right for where you are. Coordinates are used in on-device calculations and stored locally; the app never sends them anywhere. On devices with Google Play services, the location request itself is answered by Google Play services, under Google's privacy policy and your device's location settings. You can skip this entirely and enter locations manually.
  • Internet (INTERNET) — used for one thing: Android's own Geocoder service, when you search for a city by name while adding a location. That lookup is performed by the operating system, not by this app, and is subject to your device vendor's privacy policy. If it fails, the app carries on and you can enter coordinates directly. No other feature of the app uses the network. Time-zone resolution, the ephemeris and the whole panchanga engine run offline. Purchases are handled by the Google Play Store app on your device, which contacts Google itself.
  • Google Play billing (com.android.vending.BILLING) — lets the app ask the Google Play Store app to show its purchase sheet for Vedic Mitra Pro, and to say whether your Google account owns it. You enter payment details in Google Play, never in the app. The app receives no payment details: only whether the purchase is complete or pending, and a purchase token it hands back to Google Play to confirm the purchase.
  • Motion sensors — the Vastu compass reads the phone's orientation sensor while the compass is open, to show a bearing. The readings are used on the spot and are neither stored nor sent.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — to show the reminders you create.
  • Exact alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM), full-screen intent, vibrate and foreground service — to make muhurta reminders fire at the right minute and ring when you ask them to.
  • Boot completed (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) — to restore your scheduled reminders after a restart, so they are not silently lost.

Buying Vedic Mitra Pro

Pro is sold by Google Play, under the Google Play Terms of Service and Google's privacy policy. Google processes the payment and keeps the order. As the seller, the developer can see the order records Google Play gives every seller, such as order numbers, products, prices, dates and countries, and uses them only for accounting, refunds and answering questions about a purchase. The developer never receives card, bank or UPI details. Refunds and cancellations follow Google Play's policies.

Reports can also be bought one at a time, for one person or one pair. To tie a report purchase to its person, the app attaches a key to it: a keyed hash, made with a secret that never leaves your device, of which profile or pair it is for. Google keeps that key with the order, and it reveals nothing about the person: not a name, a birth date or a place. A partner's details entered for a single match report are used for that report only and are not saved. Google Play does not restore a consumed report purchase, so a reinstall or cleared app data loses it; the app suggests saving a copy of the PDF.

Sharing a report

Share as PDF, on the kundali and matchmaking screens, writes a report to the app's cache on your device and opens Android's share sheet. The report goes only where you choose to send it; after that, the receiving app's own privacy policy applies. The app keeps no record of where it went.

What the app does not do

  • No user accounts, sign-in or identifiers.
  • No analytics, telemetry or usage statistics.
  • No crash or performance reporting.
  • No advertising SDKs, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information; there is nothing to sell, and no recipient.
  • No profiling, and no automated decisions about you.

Links that leave the app

The About screen links to GitHub and LinkedIn, and the Support screen to this policy. Tapping one opens your browser and hands you to that service, where its privacy policy applies and this one stops. The app learns nothing about whether you followed a link, or what you did next.

Report a bug opens your email app with a message addressed to the developer. Nothing is sent unless you send it, and what you write is handled only to answer you.

Children

Vedic Mitra is not directed at children, and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because nothing leaves your device, the developer holds no data of yours to access, export, correct or erase. To delete everything the app holds, clear the app's storage or uninstall it. If you have emailed the developer and want that correspondence deleted, ask at the address below. Records of a purchase are held by Google; see them, or ask for a refund, through Google Play.

Third-party components

The app is built on open-source libraries; where Google Play services is present, Google's Play services Location library for the optional location feature; and the Google Play Billing Library, to buy and restore Vedic Mitra Pro. None of them is configured to send data to the developer.
