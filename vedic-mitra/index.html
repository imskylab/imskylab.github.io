What it does

Timings are calculated on the device for your coordinates, not looked up from a table.

The day

Full daily panchanga

Tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana, paksha, vara, ayana, ritu, maasa in either reckoning, samvatsara and the era years, named by the sunrise convention.

Muhurta and kalams

Brahma and Abhijit Muhurta, Dur Muhurta, Varjyam, Rahu, Yamaganda and Gulika, and all sixteen Choghadiya windows, drawn as a timeline of the day.

Sky and planets

Sunrise and sunset, moonrise and moonset, the moon's phase, and the rashi of the Sun, Moon, Guru and Shukra with their next pravesh.

Festivals and vrats

Upcoming festivals, dated by their puja window where the tradition times one, with Ekadashi, Purnima, Amavasya, Sankashti, Pradosh and the Sankrantis.

Reminders that fire

Exact alarms for muhurta, Choghadiya and tithi events that survive a restart, with your choice of lead time. With Pro, any of them can ring as a full-screen alarm.

A monthly calendar

This month and the months either side, each day with its full panchanga, festivals and seasonal routine. With Pro, any month, past or future.

Jyotish

Kundali Pro

The natal chart with seventeen divisional charts, planetary positions to the arcminute, ashtakavarga and the named yogas in the chart.

Dashas Pro

Vimshottari, Ashtottari and Yogini, three levels deep.

Matchmaking Pro

Ashtakoota across all eight kootas, the four porutham, and Mangal dosha with its classical cancellations, each with its working shown.

Choosing a time

Ranked muhurta windows for an activity, for everyone. With Pro, chosen for one person or a couple, and searched up to 90 days ahead.

Reports

A kundali or a match as a PDF, made on your phone, to print or send to family. Bought one at a time, with or without Pro; Pro includes your own kundali.

Rashifal

A daily and weekly outlook computed from Chandrabala and Tarabala, reading the transit's traditional meaning rather than inventing predictions.

Reference, cited from the texts

The samskaras

Thirteen life-cycle rites as the grhyasutras describe them, each with its passage and the muhurta for it.

Vastu

The eight quarters and the Vastu Purusha Mandala, a live compass corrected to true north, what Brihat Samhita places where, and proportion from the Manasara.

Ayurveda

The daily and seasonal routine, foods and their qualities, a prakriti questionnaire, and everyday complaints and common herbs as the texts record them.

Stotra, japa and meditation

A library of stotras and mantras, a 108-bead japa counter and a meditation timer, the last two with daily streaks.

How it is made

Three commitments that shape every screen.

Computed, and checked

The astronomy is derived from your coordinates, and calculations are cross-checked against published almanacs before release.

Cited, not invented

Anything the app says about a tradition names the text it comes from, and says so plainly where a source is not yet recorded. Outcomes and promises the texts attach are left out.

Offline and private

Your birth details, places and history stay on your phone. There are no servers, no account and no analytics. Read the privacy policy.

A note on Ayurveda. What the app shows is history and tradition, reported from named texts, and not medical advice. It gives no doses, and nothing in it replaces a doctor's care.
