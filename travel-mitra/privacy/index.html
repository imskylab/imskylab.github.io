Travel Mitra — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026

Travel Mitra is a road-trip recorder and planner for Android, created and developed by Jayvardhan Potabatti ("I", "me"). Contact: skylabs.in@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Travel Mitra has no account and no server. I do not receive your data.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no crash-reporting service.
  • Your trips stay on your phone.
  • Online lookups are optional and happen only when you ask for one. Recorded GPS history, trips and expenses are never sent.

What is stored on your phone

The app keeps the following in its private storage on your phone:

  • trips, with their recorded GPS samples, distance and times;
  • stops, trip events and planned places;
  • expenses, fuel and toll entries, and receipt photos you attach;
  • trip participants and the cost split;
  • vehicles, including the registration number if you enter it;
  • notes and settings.

Other apps cannot read this storage. The database is not encrypted by the app; it is protected by Android's app sandbox and your phone's own lock and encryption.

The app turns off Android's automatic cloud backup and device-to-device transfer of its data. If you uninstall the app or lose your phone, its data is gone unless you exported a backup archive (see "Exports and backups").

Optional online features

These features work only after they are set up in the app and only when you ask for a result. Nothing is sent in the background. All requests use HTTPS and go directly from your phone to Google.

  1. Route estimate (Google Routes API). When you ask for an estimate, the app sends the origin, destination and waypoints you entered (as coordinates or the address text you typed), the planned departure time if it is in the future, and a request for toll prices if tolls are turned on.
  2. Nearby places (Google Places API). When you search, the app sends one search point and the kind of place you chose (for example fuel or food) with a search radius. The point is either your current location rounded to about 110 metres (3 decimal places), or a place from your plan that you picked. The results are shown during that session only and are not stored.

Your recorded GPS history, trips, stops, expenses, notes, receipts and vehicles are never sent.

Like any internet request, Google receives your phone's IP address with the request. Google processes these requests under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. The data is not shared with anyone else, and I keep nothing.

Opening Google Maps. When you choose to open directions or a place in a maps app, the app passes that destination (or place) to the maps app you choose, which then handles it under its own policy.

The app does not request camera or storage permissions. Receipt photos are taken or chosen through Android's camera app or photo picker, and only when you attach a receipt.

Exports and backups

The app makes a PDF report, a CSV file or a backup archive (which includes receipt photos) only when you choose Share or Save. The PDF report can include a drawing of your route's shape and a timeline (no map and no coordinates); someone who knows the area may still recognise places from the shape. You decide where each file goes. Copies you send to other apps, people or cloud storage are outside the app's control.

Delete your data

  • One trip: open the finished trip and delete it. This removes its route, stops, events, expenses, participants, notes and receipt photos from the phone.
  • Everything: Settings → Delete all data removes every trip, vehicle, setting and receipt photo.
  • Uninstall the app, which removes all its data from the phone.

I hold no copies of your data anywhere. Files you exported or shared stay where you put them until you delete them there.

Children

Travel Mitra is intended for adults (18 and over) and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same address with a new effective date.
