Your road-trip companion, private by design

What it does

For road trips with family and friends, from a weekend drive to a long journey.

Record trips offline

Distance and time from your phone's GPS, with no internet needed. The app tells you when GPS quality was poor, so you know how far to trust a number.

Stops, suggested for you

Stops are suggested automatically from where you paused. You can rename, merge, split or remove them.

Expenses, fuel and tolls

Note what you spend, with fuel and toll entries and a fuel estimate for the trip. Attach a receipt photo if you like.

Family cost split

Add who is travelling and who paid. The app works out each share and who should pay whom to settle up.

Plan the trip

Plan your stops before you leave, and hand off to Google Maps for turn-by-turn directions.

Summary and exports

A trip summary you can share as a PDF or CSV, and a backup archive you can restore later.

Optional extras

Look up nearby fuel, food and rest stops when you ask, and turn on break reminders for long drives.

Private by design

There is no Travel Mitra server and no account.

Your trips stay on your phone

Routes, stops, expenses and notes are kept in the app's private storage on your phone. No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Online lookups only when you ask

Route estimates and nearby places are optional. They send only what that lookup needs, directly to Google, and never your recorded GPS history.

Please note

Numbers are estimates

Distances, route times, tolls and fuel figures are estimates. GPS accuracy depends on your phone and surroundings.

Don't use your phone while driving

Set up your trip before you go, and let a passenger use the app on the way.

Background recording depends on your phone

Recording with the screen off relies on Android and your phone's battery settings. Some phones stop background apps; the app shows when recording was interrupted.

Not affiliated with any company or government body
