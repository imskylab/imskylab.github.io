How we work

Engineering you can trust

The same principles run through every app we ship, ours and yours.

Accuracy, verified

Results are checked against independent references before release, and every app ships with an automated test suite.

Private by design

Offline-first and on-device by default, with encryption at rest. No servers to breach, no account to create, no analytics.

Decisions written down

Architecture decision records, release runbooks and source citations, so the product stays maintainable long after launch.

Respectful UX

Clean Material design, light and dark themes, accessible contrast and no dark patterns. Users come back because the app is useful.

Process

From idea to store, in clear stages

We build in gates. Each stage ends with a working, tested build you can review before the next one starts.

Discover

We map the domain, the users and the constraints, and agree on a written specification.

Design

Flows, screens and architecture are set out and decided before code begins.

Build & verify

Feature by feature, each gate passing its tests, analysis and on-device checks.

Launch & grow

Signed releases, store listing, privacy policy, then updates as your users ask for more.
